The tilt axis crosses the pan axis, so the camera turns about a line through its own body and stays inside the fork. What is left to check is whether it clears what is underneath it — and the sweep includes the payload, at both ends of its balance slot.

Nothing encloses the driver end. The pedestal is open on all four sides, so the OLED and its three buttons stay reachable — on firmware below v1.0.6 they are the only way to read the work mode and the current limit.
